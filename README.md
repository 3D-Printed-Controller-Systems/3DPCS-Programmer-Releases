## Getting started

1. **Download** the app for your computer from the latest release.
2. **Plug in** your controller with a USB cable.
3. **Open the app.** It finds your controller, offers any update, and shows every button as you press it.
4. **Make it yours**: calibrate, pick your colors and add mods. Your settings are saved on the controller, so they go wherever it goes.

## First time opening it

- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag the app to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
- **Linux:** right-click the file, choose **Properties**, turn on **Allow executing as a program**, then double-click it.
- **App signing:** we're setting up signing for Windows and Mac. Once it's in place, these warnings go away.

## About

The 3DPCS Controller Tool and its firmware are written by 3D Printed Controller Systems and aren't based on any other configurator. They use a few standard open-source parts (Tauri, Svelte, the Raspberry Pi Pico SDK, TinyUSB, BTstack and Pico-PIO-USB for USB port access), listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).
